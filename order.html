Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Can I customise my bouquet or hamper?
Yes, absolutely! We love creating bespoke arrangements. Contact us via WhatsApp to discuss your vision.
Do flower prices fluctuate?
Yes, prices may vary depending on the season and availability of specific blooms.
Can I cancel my order?
Yes, cancellations are accepted at least 48 hours before delivery. Please contact us as soon as possible.
Are refunds available?
Refunds may apply to eligible cancellations. Deposits are non-refundable once preparation begins.
Do you offer same-day delivery?
Yes, same-day delivery is available subject to stock availability. Please contact us early to confirm.
Can I add a personal message?
Yes, you can! We'll include your personal message with the delivery.
Do you offer delivery?
Yes, we offer delivery at an additional fee. Contact us for a quote based on your location.
What if an item is unavailable?
We'll provide a suitable alternative of equal or greater value. We'll always confirm with you first.
How do I place an order?
Contact us via WhatsApp to place your order. We'll guide you through the process.
